1. Overview

English Words: Uzbek & Russian ("the app") helps you learn English vocabulary. The app does not require an account, and we do not run a server that collects or stores your personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

Your learning progress, unlocked units and settings (such as the translation language) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, including rewarded ads that you can watch to unlock new units. Google may collect and use the following data through its SDK to serve and measure ads:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • Advertising data and ad interaction data
  • Approximate (coarse) location
  • Crash, performance and other diagnostic data

This data is used for third-party advertising, analytics and developer advertising or marketing. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Tracking permission (iOS)

On first launch, iOS asks whether the app may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, ads may be more relevant to you. If you decline, the app still works and shows ads that are not personalized using the advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored in the app is deleted when you uninstall it. Data held by Google is governed by Google's policies and controls.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with its date at the top.

8. Contact

If you have questions, contact us at meeyor2024@gmail.com.

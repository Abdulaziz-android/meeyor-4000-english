Contact

Need help or want to report a problem? Email us at meeyor2024@gmail.com . Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the issue.

How do I unlock a new unit?

The first units are free. To unlock another unit, watch one short rewarded ad. The unit then stays unlocked. An internet connection is needed to load the ad.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All words and exercises are stored in the app. Only loading a rewarded ad requires internet.

How do I change the translation language?

Open the app's Settings and choose Uzbek or Russian.

The ad does not load

Check your internet connection and try again in a few minutes. If the problem continues, email us.
